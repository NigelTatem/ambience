Ambience 1.2.0 adds built-in signed updates and a Discover tab.

- Local MP4/MOV wallpapers with audio and adjustable loops.
- Per-video zoom and positioning with a draggable preview.
- Settings for startup, battery usage, storage, and update checks.
- Discover supports resolution selection, source credits, cancellation, and verified downloads. The initial catalog is empty pending approved wallpaper uploads.

Install: download the Ambience DMG, open it, and drag Ambience to Applications. This free beta is not Apple-notarized, so macOS may require manual approval under Privacy & Security. No compiler is needed for the released app.
